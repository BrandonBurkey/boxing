the transcript, not every frame. No assets are stored in the repo.

## Visual language (cover frame)
- Dark charcoal gradient background, with a perspective floor grid in the lower third only.
- A small coloured square + tracked-out label top-left, sitting just above the headline.
- A huge condensed headline, left-aligned in the top third, wrapping over 2 lines.
- Two side-view figures standing on the floor line. They fill the middle 45% of the frame.
- The figures are flat capsule limbs. The far-side limbs are darker than the near-side ones (cheap depth).
- Height labels: a number in condensed type above a dotted line level with the top of the head.

## What we do differently (our own look)
- **Colours:** cool ice-white "YOU" with electric-lime gloves, against a deep indigo-slate opponent with
  dark gloves. They use warm cream with red.
- **Construction:** our figures have visible round joint caps, tapered limbs, a pill-shaped torso
  and a hip block.
- **Stage:** a full subtle square grid, a lit "spotlight" floor ellipse, and a ring-rope line far behind.
- **Captions:** word-by-word karaoke captions in the lower third, so the video works with sound off
  past the hook. As far as I can tell from the cover, theirs only shows the headline.
- **Extra devices:** HUD bars, a top-down "geometry" view, impact rings, a punch-count scoreboard, and
  camera push-ins on impacts.
