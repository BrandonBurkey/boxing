# Scout: baseball (hitting, pitching, fielding; softball crossover)

Checked 2026-10-27 with plain requests only. Verdict: **MAYBE, 71/100.** Baseball has the best proof of
willingness to pay of any team sport we have scouted. Parents already buy pitching and swing apps with $100+
annual tiers. We found no faceless animated technique account. But real-footage educators are big and good, and
our saturation search was cut short (see "Limits"), so the format gap is less certain than it looks.

## 4. Format fit
Hitting and pitching are **one body plus a bat or ball**. Swing plane, attack angle, hip-shoulder separation and arm
slot are pure lines and angles, and that is our strongest geometry match after boxing. Ball flight matters for
launch angle and pitch movement. It can be faked with an arc.

(https://www.baseball-reference.com/players/a/altuvjo01.shtml); Aaron Judge 6-7, 282 lb
(https://www.baseball-reference.com/players/j/judgeaa01.shtml); Shohei Ohtani 6-4, 210 lb
