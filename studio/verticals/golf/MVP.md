# Golf MVP concept: "fix one fault" practice planner (DRAFT, for founder approval)

**One line:** Pick the miss that's ruining your round. Get a 14-day plan of short range and home drills that
fixes it, each one shown with the same animation as the videos.

**Why this job, not swing analysis:** "analyse my swing on my phone" is owned by 4.9★ incumbents (Sportsbox, now
Bryson-backed; GolfFix, 1.5M+ downloads; see `SCOUT.md` §3). A tips library alone doesn't sell (Golf Secrets:
no ratings). A *structured plan for one fault* matches what the videos teach, and nobody owns it.

**Who:** adult amateur golfers with a handicap of roughly 10–30 who practise at a range or at home and have one
recurring miss (slice, fat or thin strikes, no distance).

## Core features (v1). Every video plug must name one of these.
| # | Feature | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Fault finder** | 3 questions about your ball flight (where it starts, how it curves, how you strike it) point you to the most likely fault and plan. It suggests, never diagnoses. |
| 2 | **14-day fix plans** | Slice, hook, fat/thin, distance, balance. Daily 15–30 minute sessions: drills, ball counts, an audio coach. |
| 3 | **Animated checkpoints** | Each drill opens with a down-the-line or top-down animation of the position to hit (swing plane, arc width, finish balance). |
| 4 | **Film & compare** | Record your swing on your phone and play it side by side with the animated checkpoint. No AI scoring in v1. |
| 5 | **Ball-flight log** | Tap start direction and curve for each shot. A simple chart shows whether the miss is shrinking. |

**Not in v1:** AI swing analysis, GPS/course maps, handicap tracking, sensors, putting.

**Monetisation (proposal):** first plan free; Pro £8.99/month or £59.99/year (a structured plan sits below
Sportsbox's $110/yr). Waitlist perk to be confirmed before it's promised anywhere.

**Data:** on-device, no accounts in v1. The waitlist email list is the only personal data and needs a privacy notice.

## Plug lines must map to features
- Distance / arc video → #2 (distance plan) or #3 (arc checkpoint)
- Swing plane / slice video → #3 (plane checkpoint) or #1 (fault finder)
- Balance / footwork video → #2 (balance plan) or #4 (film & compare)
