# Which sports to enter next: ranking after the 22-sport scout (2026-10-27)

> **Founder decision, 2026-10-27: individual sports only.** The format (one body vs one opponent, or one body
> fixing its own technique) and the app (solo practice) don't fit team sports, where the lessons are
> positional and players learn from a club coach. Football, American football, rugby, cricket, volleyball,
> ice hockey and baseball are **parked**. Basketball is the only borderline case (solo shooting and handles).
> **Golf is the first new vertical: build started.**
>
> Active shortlist: Golf 79 · Running 81 (needs gait animation) · MMA 77 · Muay Thai 76 · Tennis 76
> (contested) · Badminton 75 · BJJ 72 · Pickleball 71 · Swimming 71 · Climbing 69.

**Short answer:** the faceless-animated-technique format is **almost unclaimed outside boxing**. Of 22 sports,
none has a large animated technique account. One early operator is spreading the format sport by sport
(`<sport>.geometry`: tennis 4.3k, padel 2.9k, golf 678, running 0). Most sports' "tips" audiences are owned by
live-action coaches with 200k–1.2M followers. That proves the demand exists, but none of them animate.

**Recommended order:**
1. **MMA + Muay Thai**: zero engine work. Same rig and moves as boxing.
2. **Golf**: richest buyers, open format.
3. **Basketball**: biggest audience and biggest names, open format.
4. **Running**: top raw score, but needs a gait-cycle animation first.

## Confidence warning
The studio's shared web-search budget ran out partway through the scan. Most sports got 3–10 mechanic searches,
not the 8+ the brief requires. Web search barely indexes TikTok, so "OPEN" means "nothing found", not "nothing
exists". The handle and profile numbers below were fetched directly and are solid.
**Founder to-do before any launch:** spend 10 minutes per chosen sport searching inside the TikTok app
("<sport> animation", "<star> explained", "<sport> tips animated"). One 50k+ animated account in a sport
moves it to CONTESTED (about −8 points).

## Strategy calls
1. **Combat first (MMA + Muay Thai).** It's the cheapest win. The rig, poses, hit FX and scripts all transfer, and
   a pilot can ship this week. Threat: the **JAB AI** app (4.7★, 506 ratings) already covers boxing, Muay Thai,
   MMA, BJJ and wrestling, and has a TikTok (@jab.ai, 15.6k). JabMax is still boxing-only (89.1k, 20 videos).
   Decision needed: one "combat sports" app and account, or one per sport?
2. **Golf is the money vertical.** Buyers are adults who already pay (Sportsbox 4.9★/3.7k ratings, up to
   $110/yr). But swing *analysis* is taken, so our app must sell a different job: drills and a fix-of-the-day
   tied to famous swings. Move before @golf.geometry grows.
3. **Basketball is the reach vertical.** It has the biggest audience and names, and no animated incumbent. HomeCourt
   ($7.99/mo, 4.8★, NBA partner) is the app to beat, so willingness to pay is middling.
4. **Running scores highest but needs the most new animation** (a convincing gait cycle). The app market is owned
   by Strava/Runna. Our angle would be form coaching (rival: Ochy). Build it after golf.
5. **Watch the .geometry operator.** They are early (under 5k everywhere) but clearly copying the format across
   sports. Speed matters more than polish in golf and tennis.
6. **Park:** cricket (TikTok is banned in India), rugby (tackle-advice liability, low willingness to pay), padel,
   table tennis, wrestling and skateboarding.
