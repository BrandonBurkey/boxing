# Scout: American football (position technique + flag)

Checked 2026-10-27 with plain requests only. Verdict: **MAYBE, 69/100.** Nobody we could find makes faceless
animated *technique* explainers for football positions, and the US playing base is huge. Flag football is growing
fast. But player-training apps show almost no paying demand, the audience is mostly teens, and our search
coverage was thin (see "Limits" below).

## 4. Format fit
Football technique is mostly **two bodies at the line**: a WR against a DB, an OL against a DL. That is our boxing
setup, with leverage instead of reach. QB content needs ball flight. Scheme content needs 11 bodies, so we avoid it.

Athlete facts to cite (all [verified-fetch] from Wikipedia; the fact-checker must confirm them before render):
