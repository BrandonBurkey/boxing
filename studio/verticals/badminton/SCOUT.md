# Badminton: scout report (second-tier, lighter pass)

**Verdict: MAYBE (leaning GO; the best fit of the four). Total 75/100. Saturation: OPEN for the animated format (low confidence).**
Badminton footwork is top-down geometry: six corners, a base, step counts. One body and a simple shuttle.
That fits our engine best of the four sports. There is also proof of willingness to pay: **Badminton Famly**
(72.8k on TikTok, a world champion coach) already runs the TikTok-to-paid-app funnel with live-action video,
at $59.90–$119.90 a year. They prove the money, and they are the competitor for sign-ups.

## 4. Format fit
Hooks. Facts need the fact-check pass; note that Axelsen is now retired.
1. "Slow feet don't lose rallies. Not returning to base does." (Top-down base and corners.) Lin Dan.
2. "Weak arms don't kill your smash. Contacting the shuttle behind your head does." (Contact-point line.) Axelsen.
3. "Being short doesn't lose the net. Lunging with the wrong foot does." (Lunge-angle line.) Lee Chong Wei.
4. "You're not too slow for the back corner. You're running when you should scissor-jump." (Step-count top-down.)
5. "Your clear doesn't fall short from a weak arm. It falls short because you're square to the net." (Shoulder-angle line.) Lin Dan.
6. "Big smashes don't win points. Steep ones do." (Smash-angle device; a simple shuttle line.) Axelsen.
7. "You aren't late to the shuttle. Your split step is." (Timing bar.)
8. "Your backhand isn't weak. Your grip never changed." (Grip inset.)
9. "Six corners, one base. Most players use four." (Top-down footwork map.)
10. "Doubles isn't about smashing. It's about who covers the gap." (Top-down rotation.) **2+ bodies.**

**Props/rigs:** racket (one-hand attachment), shuttle (a simple straight or arc line, no spin), net, court
lines (top-down, with six-corner markers), and lunge and scissor-jump poses. Mostly reusable top-down devices.
