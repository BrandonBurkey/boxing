## 4. Format fit: 10 hooks
All are 2 bodies, no ball. **Every hook needs ground poses.** Facts are [unverified] until fact-checked.
1. "Being smaller doesn't keep you stuck under mount. Lying flat on your back does." Roger Gracie (mount). Hip-angle device.
2. "Weak arms don't get you armbarred. Your elbow leaving your ribs does." Roger Gracie. Elbow-to-rib distance line.
3. "Escaping mount isn't strength. It's one bridge at the right angle." Top-down angle.
4. "Getting passed isn't speed. Your knee lining up with his hip is." Marcelo Garcia. Top-down line.
5. "Strong grips don't stop the back take. Turtling facing the wrong way does." Gordon Ryan. Top-down arrow.
6. "Triangles don't fail on big guys. They fail when your angle is square." Top-down angle.
7. "You don't lose the guillotine by tapping late. You lose it when your head goes under his arm." Marcelo Garcia. Side view.
8. "Bad flexibility doesn't lose your guard. Feet on the mat with no frame does." Mikey Musumeci. Distance line.
9. "Side control doesn't hold you down. Shrimping the wrong way does." Top-down arrow.
10. "Heel hooks don't need strength. They need the knee line." Craig Jones. Line device. **Injury-risk topic. Cut, or add a safety caveat.**

**Props and rigs needed:** a mat; lying, seated, turtle and kneeling poses; leg-entanglement IK; limb
occlusion/draw order for two grounded bodies; a top-down view as the primary camera; optional gi (no-gi first is cheaper).
