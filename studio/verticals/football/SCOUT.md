# Football (soccer) scout

Checked 2026-10-27. TikTok data came from plain `curl` of public profile, video and tag pages, parsing
`__UNIVERSAL_DATA_FOR_REHYDRATION__`. I used no spoofed user agent and no headless browser.

## Verdict: MAYBE, 70/100. Enter only with individual technique, never tactics.

- **Tactics and analysis animation is SATURATED.** Tifo Football by The Athletic has 588k followers.
  Coaches' Voice, The Tactics Board and Football XI's also work this space.
- **Animated, faceless individual technique: no credible account found.** First touch, body shape,
  scanning, 1v1s and defending are all taught on TikTok, but by **real coaches on camera**. Several have
  45k–209k followers, and they already sell an app or a course from the bio. That is our funnel, minus
  the animation.
- **Why only MAYBE.** The format gap may be real, but our search could not see inside TikTok. Football
  technique also needs a ball, and usually a third body (a goalkeeper or a passer). That makes it the
  costliest engine port so far. The audience skews young, which is a data-protection problem for the
  waitlist.
- **Gaps in this report.** The shared web-search budget ran out partway through. UK and US participation
  figures and MOTI Soccer's App Store page were not checked. Hashtag stats were not present in the tag
  pages.
